## End-of-task status

When you finish a task in which you changed, or tried to change, something
(files, commits, settings, deployments...), end your last reply with a short
status block, so the user sees at a glance what happened and what is expected
of them:

- **Done**: what was done
- **Committed/pushed**: branch + hash, and whether it is committed locally
  only or also pushed; or "no"
- **Not done / blocked**: what is left and why, or "nothing"
- **Waiting on user**: yes/no + what you need from them

Write it in the user's language (the labels may be translated). Leave it out
for plain questions and answers that changed nothing. If this project's own
instructions ask for another report format, follow those instead.
